A first-of-its-kind studio for the last read of a paper

Writing tools help you write; review systems help editors judge. Nothing was built for the moment in between, when co-authors give a finished manuscript its final read. Research Studio brings together paragraph-by-paragraph reading, attention checks, verification of every number against the paper's own tables and figures (figures included, read back into data), full history with undo, and write-back into your LaTeX source with a latexdiff-style diff.

How it works

From finished draft to fixed manuscript

  1. 1

    Upload

    Drop a PDF or your LaTeX project. It is split — in your browser — into paragraphs, sections, equations, tables, figures and references.

  2. 2

    Invite

    Invite co-authors by email as proofreaders or editors. An invitation lets exactly that address create an account and join.

  3. 3

    Read & suggest

    Readers see each paragraph as typeset, suggest edits with live diffs, comment, mark text and answer the occasional reading check.

  4. 4

    Fix & export

    Accept, merge or reject — every change is versioned and can be undone. Write changes into your .tex files, export a diff, the revised project, an annotated PDF or a review report.

In this edition

Everything runs on your server — no AI service needed

∑

Evidence graph

Numbers in the text are matched to the table row and column they come from, or to the plotted data, and flagged when they differ. The same quantity reported differently in two places is flagged too.

◩

Figures as data

Charts are read back into numbers from the vector drawing (multi-panel figures panel by panel, box plots included), from pgfplots source, by OCR, or with a click-to-digitise tool.

?

Reading checks

Short fill-in-the-blank questions built from the paragraph itself. Owners see pass rates and implausibly fast reading per reader.

⚑

Consistency checks

Unresolved references, undefined or twice-defined acronyms, missing figures and tables, mixed spellings, duplicated sentences, placeholder text.

⇄

LaTeX write-back

Accepted changes go into your .tex files at their exact positions, into a local folder or a revised .zip. Bring an edited version back and every edit is merged into the history.

✓

Private by design

Built-in accounts, invitation-only projects, AES-256-GCM encryption at rest, watermarked page images for readers, and an audit trail for owners.

AI proofreading, reference checking with RefCheck and the round trip with Rykhet LaTeX run on the hosted demo at studio.rykhet.com.
